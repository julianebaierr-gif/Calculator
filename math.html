Mathematical Principles

Number Theory, Proportionality & Discrete Arithmetic

By CalcHub Pure & Applied Mathematics Editorial Board • Verified against Standard Algebraic Proofs

Arithmetic precision is foundational to science, engineering, and everyday economics. Floating-point errors in generic calculators frequently generate deceptive inaccuracies. CalcHub employs exact integer algorithms for rational numbers and proportional mathematics.

1. Euclid's Greatest Common Divisor (GCD) Algorithm

The Euclidean algorithm computes the greatest common divisor of two integers: given numbers \(a\) and \(b\), \(\gcd(a, b) = \gcd(b, a \pmod b)\). This recursive reduction powers both fraction simplification and ratio irreducible form determinations.

2. Proportional Scaling & Direct Variation

In chemistry, architectural drafting, and recipe formulation, ratios govern equivalent relationships. When maintaining proportion \(A : B = C : D\), the product of the means equals the product of the extremes: \(A \times D = B \times C\).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between a ratio and a fraction?
A ratio compares two parts to each other (e.g., 2 parts flour to 1 part sugar), whereas a fraction typically represents a part-to-whole relationship (e.g., 2/3 of the mixture is flour).
How does credit weighting affect GPA?
A 4-credit course exerts double the mathematical leverage on your cumulative Grade Point Average compared to a 2-credit course because quality points are multiplied by credit hours before dividing by total credits.
